About the website

What is this website for?

This website serves as a recommendation hub for Filipino-made guitars. While there is an abundance of local options, only a few brands and models deliver reliable quality and genuine value for money. This site highlights those select instruments.

Can I purchase guitars directly from this website?

No. The website does not sell guitars or accessories. It is an independent recommendation platform with no affiliations or commercial ties with the brands mentioned.

How credible are the opinions on this website?

The insights provided are based on personal experience. The site's creator has been playing guitar since 2017, with several years of hands-on experience evaluating instruments.

Are the reviews sponsored?

No. All recommendations are made independently. The website is not influenced by paid sponsorships, partnerships, or advertisements.

What types of guitars are featured?

The focus is on Filipino-made guitars and models available locally. The goal is to highlight instruments that balance affordability with reliable performance.

Can readers suggest guitars or brands for review?

Yes. Suggestions are welcome and may be considered for future reviews, depending on availability and access.

About the Creator

  • 2017 – The journey began with an acoustic guitar, where the creator first learned the fundamentals of playing and developed an appreciation for music. This year was dedicated to building a foundation of technique, rhythm, and passion.
  • 2018 – After gaining confidence, the creator purchased their first electric guitar. This milestone opened doors to a broader range of styles and genres, allowing experimentation with tones and techniques unique to the electric instrument.
  • 2019 – Live performance became an integral part of the creator's growth. Playing in shows provided valuable experience in front of audiences and helped hone stage presence and adaptability as a musician.
  • 2020 – A deep interest in blues music emerged. The creator explored its history, techniques, and emotional depth, adding expressive elements such as bending, vibrato, and improvisation into their playing.
  • 2021 – With an eagerness to grow further, the creator began exploring multiple guitars. This also included learning about how specific guitars are suited to particular styles, from rock and jazz to funk and beyond.
  • 2022 – Songwriting became a focus. The creator started composing original music, intertwining personal experiences and diverse influences into pieces that reflected individuality as an artist.
  • 2023 – Following the challenges of the pandemic, the creator returned to performing in shows. This re-entry into the live scene strengthened their connection with audiences and revitalized the excitement of playing on stage.
  • 2024 – Performance opportunities expanded further with participation in a Battle of the Bands competition. This tested both skill and creativity in a high-pressure environment, ultimately sharpening musicianship and confidence.
  • 2025 – By this year, the creator had performed with numerous guitars across different venues and settings. This extensive, first-hand experience contributed to a well-rounded knowledge of instruments, their strengths, and their suitability for various playing contexts.
